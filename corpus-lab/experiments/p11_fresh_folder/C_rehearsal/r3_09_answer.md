## Federal development spending, last ten years

Two measures are held on the shelf, both consistent across the *Pakistan Economic Survey 2025‑26* and *Annual Budget Statement 2025‑26* — but both go stale before the present day, so the "ten years" window is 2010‑11/2014‑15 through 2021‑22/2023‑24, not through 2025‑26.

**Federal PSDP (Public Sector Development Programme), Rs million** — narrower, Planning Commission allocation figure:
- 2010‑11: 268,933 → 2011‑12: 296,841 → 2012‑13: 308,680 → 2013‑14: 349,274 → 2014‑15: 358,577 → 2015‑16: 405,090 → 2016‑17: 421,159 → 2017‑18: 501,500 → 2018‑19: 499,809 → 2019‑20: 540,402 → 2020‑21: 660,116 (**revised**) → 2021‑22: 658,266 (**provisional**)

Roughly a 2.4× rise over the period, though it plateaus 2020‑21→2021‑22. Both the *Economic Survey 2025‑26* and the *Annual Budget Statement 2025‑26* carry the identical series and neither has been extended past 2021‑22 in any edition checked (2024‑25 or 2025‑26) — this appears to be the last vintage available for this specific line, not a gap in what I looked at.

**Development expenditure and net lending, Rs million** — the broader federal development-spending aggregate (Ministry of Finance):
- 2016‑17: 1,099,000 → 2017‑18: 1,289,000 → 2018‑19: 1,341,000 → 2019‑20: 1,377,000 → 2020‑21: 1,557,000 → 2021‑22: 1,723,000 → 2022‑23: 2,142,108 (**revised**) → 2023‑24: 2,852,277 (**provisional**)

This broader measure roughly 2.6× over 2016‑17–2023‑24, with a sharp step‑up in the last two years shown (2022‑23 revised, 2023‑24 provisional).

I did not find budget/allocation figures for 2022‑23 through 2025‑26 in either document under these table numbers — if you need the most recent two to three years, I can dig further into the *consolidated federal and provincial fiscal operations* or *demands for grants* families, which weren't fully checked.
