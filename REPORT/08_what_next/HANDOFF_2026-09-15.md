# Handoff — caption retrieval, the first live battery, and the first end-to-end run, 2026-09-15

Seven experiments were run tonight on the corrected harness. Counts only below; no paths, no
titles, nothing from the answer key. **Six pre-registered adoption gates were read and all six
STOPped or FAILED. No bar was moved and no denominator was changed to rescue a result.** Two
things nevertheless changed a great deal, and neither is a score.

---

**1. What improved.**
One number, and it does not clear its gate. Adding a caption-line retrieval channel over all
**89,380** harvested captions takes the right document into the top ten from **9 to 10 of 17**
(NEW). Below the top ten the same channel is the largest document-channel gain this project
has measured, and it is **confirmed on the 30-question holdout**: recall@20 16 → 20, recall@50
24 → 28, and recall@100 **28 → 29**, the first time that last number has moved at all. The
trajectory walk also improved, from a metric that measured the wrong thing to **strict 12 of
24 / tolerant 14 of 24** under a vintage-tolerant rule (WEAK). Everything else got worse or
stayed still.

**3. Experiment C, and the fusion rule frozen.**
The 2026-09-14 handoff called this the best-supported untried idea in the repository: on 12 of
17 questions *some* single rewrite alone already puts the right document in its own top ten,
while all six fused manage 9. Scoring each publication by its **best** rank across the
rewrites instead of by summed reciprocal rank scores **5 of 17** — four questions worse than
the fusion it was meant to replace. The reason is simple once seen: best-rank gives every
rewrite a free top slot, so six rewrites are six chances for a wrong neighbour to take first
place, while summation is slow to be convinced and that slowness was doing the work. **The
fusion rule is frozen at `rrf`.** An aggregate diagnostic recorded with it: of the 661
publications that outrank the right one, **586 are other dated statistical series and 75 are
undated one-offs** — the system confuses a yearbook with another yearbook, not with a memo.

**4. E1 and E2, and whether the caption line is the better unit.**
E1 (lexical) 10/17 dev and 14/30 holdout. E2 (adding caption embeddings) 9/17 and 13/30 — the
only configuration all night to fall *below* the baseline, because dense retrieval dilutes a
short institutional table title with semantic neighbours. **The joint diagnostic answers the
question the experiment was really asking, and the answer is no.** Taking the union of each
question's top-20 caption hits across all six rewrites — up to 120 (file, page) pairs — a right
evidence address appears in it on **1 of 13** questions, under both E1 and E2. The caption line
is a *better unit for finding the publication and a worse one for finding the page*. It does
not do both at once, which is what "the better retrieval unit for table questions" would have
required.

**5. B2b by type, and the trajectory pattern.**
B2b came out **identical to B2 at every one of the 57 addresses** — same 42/57, same 39.7%,
zero addresses changed, and the year filter dropped zero pages. The hypothesis was that
requiring the fiscal year in the caption was throwing away the year-asking questions. It was
not. On all **6** year-asking questions, **no caption in the correct document contains the
question's subject words at all**, with or without the year. All 112 caption hits in the whole
measurement come from the seven questions that name no year, and the trajectory-only pattern
therefore held exactly: trajectory 40/42, every other type 2/15, unchanged. Because B2b did not
reach WEAK, its conditional holdout page evaluation was **not** run.

**6. The frozen configuration, in one line.**
`fusion=rrf caption_channel=lex page=first` — E1, the highest development recall@10 of the four
candidates. Note this is a configuration that STOPped its own adoption gate: choosing what the
live battery runs on and deciding what counts as an improvement are two different decisions,
and the FREEZE rule is stated over the development count.

**7. The live battery.**
Model **`claude-sonnet-5`** (the account default, read from the probe's init event). **35 of 35
sessions** used: 1 auth probe, 2 isolation probes, the frozen 20, 12 extra absence questions.
**32 of 32 battery sessions completed, 0 timeouts, $8.53.** Both isolation probes passed — a
planted 16-hex token and a listing of the private tree were both refused. 18 planted files
unchanged, 0 memory files written, rung torn down clean.

- surfaced **8/17** · opened any evidence file **2/17** · opened the right page **1/17** ·
  cited the right page **0/17** · cited a file never opened **5** · ungrounded figures **1** ·
  `absence_ok2` **1/15** and **0/3** on the frozen three.
- Behaviour gate **FAIL**. Absence gate **FAIL**.

**Did the agent open what it was handed, did it cite only what it opened, did it refuse
honestly?** In plain words:

*It opened things — all seventeen sessions ran `open` commands, fifty-six of them, and twelve
of seventeen wrote a note before answering. It very largely cited only what it opened: just two
questions named a file it had never looked at. So the rule we spent two nights wanting to test
was followed. **It opened the wrong pages.** On six of the eight questions where the right file
was printed on its screen, it went and opened something else instead. The discipline is not the
problem; choosing which of the ranked candidates is worth opening is the problem, and that sits
one step before any rule about citation.*

*On refusing honestly, the system and the scorer disagree, and the disagreement is the story.
Fourteen of the fifteen absence sessions ran the shelf, got its verdict that we do not hold
that edition, and printed it. The scorer credits one. It requires a decline phrase and no
numbers in the same answer, and each half fails separately — eight answers quote no figures but
do not use a phrase the regex recognises, and six decline in substance while quoting the
shelf's own count of which editions we do hold, which the scorer reads as an invented figure.*

The first of the three pre-registered readings applies: **surfaced is comparatively high while
opened_right_page is near zero, so the loss sits after retrieval.** Live retrieval did not
underperform the offline gate by much — offline top-ten is 10 of 17, the agent's own rewrites
surfaced 8 of 17 — so the agent writes rewrites about as well as Haiku does.

**8. S2, ED2, and whether the architecture was redrawn.**
S2 strict **12 of 24**, tolerant **14 of 24**, mean candidate set 2.46; of the 14 hits the
edition that answered was the year itself 4 times, **one year later 6 times and two years later
4 times**, with 10 years unanswered by any edition within the window. ED2 selects the right
edition set on **3 of 10** year-bearing questions with a mean set size of 2.0 — a small,
confident, wrong set, so the selection signal is absent rather than merely diluted. The redraw
condition required E1/E2 weak **and** ED2 ≥8/10. **The architecture was not redrawn**, and the
measured reason replaces it: the table that prints a year lives in an edition the year does not
name, so locating the table would not determine the edition even if the caption could locate it.

**9. CHAIN.**
The whole pipeline ran end to end for the first time, deterministically, with every cell
checked by the geometry-aware verifier against what the PDF actually prints rather than against
the key: **right publication in the top 5 on 8 of 17, a right (file, page) reached on 4, and the
cell verified on 3**; absence routed correctly on **1 of 3**; 478 verifier calls, 46 pages
opened per question on average. The decomposition in one sentence: **half the loss is the
document box as expected, and the other half is new — the pipeline throws away half of the
questions its own document retrieval got right, at the edition-and-page step, while VERIFY
itself converted 3 of the 4 addresses it was given and was not the bottleneck.**

**10. Each box.**
*Keep:* SHELF; VERIFY, which converted 3 of 4 in the chain and has still never failed a gate;
session isolation, now tested adversarially with two live probes. *Weak:* the caption channel
(real below the top ten, nothing at it); the trajectory walk at the bottom of its band; ROUTE's
presence half at 8/11; **the open-before-cite rule — followed, and worth almost nothing on its
own**. *Failed:* RETRIEVE DOCUMENT, now a fourth and fifth way; edition selection, now twice;
the year-aware page rule; **CLAUDE ANSWERS as measured live, 0 of 17 correct citations**.
*Still untested:* the EXTRACT router; the VERIFY → retry edge; whether a richer caption harvest
would fix the coverage problem B2b exposed.

**11. The biggest remaining bottleneck, in one sentence.**
Still finding the right document — but the live battery and the chain together have sharpened
it into something narrower: the ranked list is good enough to contain the answer and not good
enough to make the answer worth opening first, and no rule about how to cite, and no reweighting
of these candidates, reaches that.

**12. The next experiment.**
**For the first time in four nights it is not a retrieval idea.** Every retrieval lever has now
been pulled and measured. The most valuable unmeasured thing in the repository is whether the
one box we call solved actually is. **Experiment F: repair the live scorer against the two
defects named in F50 — a decline regex written for a different battery, and counting a figure
quoted from the shelf's own output as an invented figure — write the gate first, then re-read
the 32 transcripts already on disk.** No model calls, no index, no retrieval change.
Pre-registered gate: **PASS** at repaired `absence_ok2` ≥10/15 *and* every credited answer
containing the shelf's own verdict string; **WEAK** 8–9/15 on the same condition; **STOP** at
≤7/15 or any answer credited without the verdict string. Second half, not optional: re-derive
the published 11/11 using the same `do_have` call the chain router uses, on the same three
frozen absence questions the chain scored 1 of 3. Two independent measurements now disagree
with that 11/11 and the disagreement cannot both be a scorer artefact — the chain's router has
no scorer in it.

Do **not**, to get past this: widen the decline regex by reading tonight's answers and adding
their phrases one at a time; count a non-declining answer as a decline because it clearly means
it; or re-run the battery with a different prompt.

**Four deviations, recorded rather than silently resolved.**

1. **`run_harness.py` could not run the specified battery.** The phase fixed a session
   configuration identical for every question, with the question text alone as the prompt and a
   six-tool disallowed list. `run_harness.py` appends a citation instruction to every question
   and does not forward `--disallowed`, and it is a frozen instrument. A new
   `c_live_battery.py` calls `ask.py` per question with exactly the specified arguments at
   `--parallel 2`; `run_harness.py` was not modified.
2. **One holdout look was spent on an inert configuration.** E1's first run reproduced the
   baseline at every depth on both sets, which was a bug signature, not a result: the caption
   list was entering fusion as publication names while fusion runs in document space, so every
   caption entry was silently dropped. Fixed and re-run. The inert run is still counted against
   the five-look budget even though it measured the already-published baseline.
3. **S2's denominator was corrected before its gate was read.** The measurement first ran over
   all 42 evidence addresses carrying a fiscal year and scored 15/42. The gate's 24 counts a
   different unit — distinct (question, fiscal year) pairs, of which there are exactly 24.
   Regrouped onto that unit and re-run; the gate band itself was untouched.
4. **The model was resolved without spending a session.** The phase asked for the account's
   default model read from a probe run with no `--model` override, but `ask.py` always passes
   one, and a second probe would have made 36 sessions against a cap of 35. The default was
   read from the user settings (`"model": "sonnet"`) and confirmed against the resolved id in
   the probe's own init event: `claude-sonnet-5`, the same model the probe and every battery
   session ran on.
