## The five questions, in this order

### 1. The honest negative

> what was the wheat production in punjab in 2015-16

**What to point out.** The folder holds the *national* figure and no provincial breakdown.
A good answer says so, names the editions it does hold, and cites what it checked — rather
than handing over the national number as though it were Punjab's. This is the behaviour the
whole thing was built around and it is the part that measures best: 11 of 11 on
document-level absence, 4 of 4 on specific identifiers.

If he pushes ("are you sure?"), let him — watch whether it holds the line or invents a
breakdown. That is the real test.

### 2. Comparability across a decade

> has pakistan's tax-to-gdp ratio improved over the last decade, and what should i be
> careful about when comparing the years

**What to point out** (Session Log A.5.1). Not the trend — the *caveats*. Watch for it
flagging that the denominator was rebased, that a series break makes the early and late
years not directly comparable, and which vintage each figure is. A tool that answers this
question without mentioning any of that is the dangerous kind.

### 3. The same number meaning three different things

> why do the figures for federal development spending in 2019-20 differ between documents,
> and which should i use

**What to point out** (Session Log A.5.2). There are genuinely several defensible figures
here — different coverage, different vintage. A good answer lays them side by side with a
page citation each and explains the difference, instead of silently picking one. In one
earlier run Opus also caught a planted file whose values had been perturbed.

### 4. A trajectory across editions

> how does sindh's annual development programme look over the last decade or so

**What to point out** (Session Log A.3). Whether it walks the editions with `series` rather
than guessing; whether it refuses to splice across a rebasing; whether every year in its
table carries its own file and page. Note the question names no file, no table and no year —
working out which publication would print this is the job being done for you.

### 5. One of his own

Let him ask anything about the folder. This is the only part of the demo that is not
rehearsed, and it is the part worth the most.

If it fails, let it fail visibly and say what happened. The project's record is a list of
things that were measured and did not work; one more is not a problem, and covering one up
would be.

## What to look for in every answer

- **A `Sources` block at the end** — one line per figure, with the path, the page and the
  verbatim line. A figure without a source line is a bug; there is a guard that is supposed
  to catch exactly that.
- **The `COVERAGE` line** — it says how many files could not be read at all. In this folder
  1,211 files are image-only scans and 32 failed to parse. If an obvious document seems
  missing, that is why.
- **Disagreement shown, not resolved silently** — two editions printing different numbers
  for the same year is normal and should be reported as such.
