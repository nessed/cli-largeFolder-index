### What this obliges us to stop saying

The pre-registered rule binds: *a difference smaller than the observed range may not be used to
prefer a configuration.* The published Sonnet-versus-Opus gap is **1**. It is inside the noise
under v3 and far inside it under v2. **"Opus is better at citation than Sonnet" is retired** —
not reversed, retired: the experiment could not tell. The same test applies to every
stack-to-stack comparison in earlier phases that turned on one or two questions.

Note the v2 range of 5 specifically. The instrument that produced every Phase 9 gate verdict
swings by five questions out of seventeen when nothing changes at all.

## Open defects, none fixed tonight

- **`c_score_live_v3.py` cannot see a session killed by an account quota.** It knows max-turns,
  timeout and empty output; a quota kill arrives as returncode 1 carrying ordinary assistant text,
  so it is silently scored as a wrong answer. `sessions_lost` reads **0** for P10O1 beside
  accounting that reads **17 of 20**. v3 is frozen at its tag and section 7 forbids changing it for
  any reason, so it was not touched — **this goes to v4**, together with the other v3 defects found
  during Gate T. The denominator is reported beside it, from the driver records.
- **The canonical export should drop surrogate row ids** before hashing. Doing so would have made
  Gate R1 pass all 8 rows on evidence it already has. Deliberately not done tonight: changing an
  export to make a gate pass, after seeing the gate fail, is the exact move the whole plan exists
  to prevent. Change the export first, re-run R1 clean, and let it read what it reads.
- **The installer creates an empty `_private/` directory** in the package root. Harmless, untidy,
  and it invites exactly the wrong assumption about what the package contains.
- **Probe sessions record no cost.**
